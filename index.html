Ecosystem Key Concepts

Three Core Divisions

  • BLV Experiences: In-person events that build trust and relationships
  • BLV Media: Content production and distribution agency
  • BLV Systems: Software infrastructure enabling scale

BLV Systems Segments

  • Creators/IP Holders: Partners bringing distribution in exchange for infrastructure
  • Companies: Internal clients paying for knowledge management systems
  • Foundations/Nonprofits: Beneficiaries receiving subsidized or donated access

Distribution Model

  • Creators function as distribution channels, not traditional customers
  • Each creator brings their existing audience to the platform
  • Trust transfers from creator to platform
  • Network effects compound as creators cross-pollinate audiences

Value Moat

  • Integration of experiences, media, and systems creates non-replicable ecosystem
  • Each component feeds and strengthens the others
  • Cannot be replicated by software, events, agencies, or creators alone
  • Switching costs increase over time through content, community, and data
